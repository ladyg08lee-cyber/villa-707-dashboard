Privacy Policy
Effective date: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be displayed, entered, stored, accessed, or otherwise handled through the Villa 707 Document Dashboard (the “Dashboard”). The Dashboard is intended for project document-control, coordination, monitoring, and handover-related purposes.

1. Information Covered

The Dashboard may contain project information such as document names, folder structures, status, dates, responsible parties, remarks, close-out information, authority-approval information, snagging and de-snagging records, testing and commissioning records, as-built information, warranties, O&M records, handover records, and other project-related data.

2. Purpose of Processing

Information is used only for legitimate project administration and document-control purposes, including tracking submissions, monitoring progress, coordinating handover requirements, maintaining records, and supporting authorized project stakeholders.

3. Access and Confidentiality

Users must access and use the Dashboard only where they are authorized to do so. Project information must not be copied, published, forwarded, disclosed, or made available to unauthorized persons. Users are responsible for protecting any credentials, links, devices, or accounts used to access project information.

4. Local Browser Storage

Where the Dashboard uses browser storage or similar local functionality, edits may be stored on the user’s own device or browser. Such local data may not automatically synchronize across users or devices unless a separate approved cloud-storage or backend service has been configured.

5. Third-Party Services

The Dashboard may rely on third-party services such as GitHub Pages, Google services, cloud storage, or other approved project systems. Those services operate under their own privacy, security, and service terms. Use of a third-party service does not change the confidentiality obligations applicable to Villa 707 project information.

6. Data Accuracy

Reasonable efforts should be made to keep Dashboard information current and accurate. Users who identify an error should report or correct it through the approved project process. The Dashboard is a tracking and coordination tool and is not, by itself, the authoritative contractual record unless expressly designated as such.

7. Retention

Project information should be retained only for as long as required for project delivery, handover, defects-liability obligations, legal or contractual requirements, audit needs, or the organization’s approved records-retention procedures.

8. Security

Appropriate administrative and technical safeguards should be used to protect project information. Users must not attempt to bypass access controls, introduce malicious code, scrape restricted information, or interfere with the operation or security of the Dashboard.

9. Personal Data

If personal information is entered into the Dashboard, it should be limited to what is necessary for legitimate project purposes and handled in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements and internal company procedures.

10. Updates to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Dashboard, project requirements, company procedures, or applicable requirements. The version displayed on the Dashboard should be treated as the current version.

11. Contact and Administration

Questions regarding access, corrections, privacy, retention, or use of Dashboard information should be directed to the authorized fäm Interior project or document-control representative.
